# Bibliography and source scope

The manuscript cites 31 distinct scholarly entries: 12 TPDS articles, 18 other formally published papers/chapters, and one explicitly identified 2017 preprint. Every entry is used for a specific technical or model-comparison role. There is no numeric journal reference minimum established by the accessible evidence. Thirty-one is the resulting relevance-first bibliography, not a claimed publisher threshold.

`bibliography-audit.csv` records each entry's actual bibliographic fields, primary publication or author source, precise passage, reading scope and manuscript role. A working DOI or a search hit is not treated as a full-paper reading. In particular, the Horn and Fenwick origin citations have primary metadata/abstract evidence for their limited attribution; they are not added to the full-text calibration totals. The proofs and implementations of the project-specific uses are supplied here.

The retained calibration remains twelve full TPDS journal texts or author-hosted journal manuscripts, six adjacent-venue readings, and five influential foundations. They are not disjoint populations: Coral and PBS serve both adjacent-venue and influential roles. The five influential readings are Coral, PBS, TACT, Dynamo and Spanner; no award is invented. The twelve TPDS texts are TTL consistency, GeWave, cooperative proxy caching, update scheduling, ASC/PAB, the weak-consistency specification framework, IRM, EAD, tree placement, minimal-cost dynamic web replication, Hint-K and SD3. The article-specific pattern matrix is in the project research plan; source/model boundaries needed by the standalone repository are retained in `literature-boundary.md`.

The temporal-connectivity citation now uses the verified SAND 2022 publication, whose publisher explicitly identifies the read arXiv paper as its full version. A later 2026 journal record was found but its full text was not verified. The continuous-churn register is still cited as the actual 2017 preprint read: a 2019 journal record is not silently substituted or counted as a thirteenth full TPDS reading. Recent self-stabilizing snapshots and reachability-under-perturbation papers are used only for their checked model distinctions, not as fully read additional calibration papers.

Scholarly texts are read-only references, not experiment inputs, and are not bundled for redistribution. No external paper's private traces, deployment results or code performance are transferred to this artifact. Reproduction is entirely offline with original generated inputs. The project-side `paper/check_bibliography.py` checks local citation, audit-field and compiled-bibliography consistency; it does not automatically verify source truth or novelty and is not a dependency of this standalone repository.

A route fixes its vertex sequence before delay choices; this is different from both delay-robust connectivity and the present joint stale-query certificate. Proofs deferred by that article to its full version were not read, and this partial reading does not increase the adjacent full-paper calibration count.
